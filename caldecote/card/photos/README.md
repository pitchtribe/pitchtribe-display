# Caldecote's photographs

Drop the club's own pictures in this folder — `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, `.webp`, `.avif` or
`.gif`. Nothing
else to do: the wall takes a dozen a day from whatever is here, reshuffled daily, woven
between the football rather than bolted on the end.

**The pool is indifferent to how many arrive.** Twelve works and four hundred works, with
the same code and no configuration. The difference is on the wall rather than in the
software: twelve is wallpaper by Thursday, four hundred is a screen that is different every
day for a season.

Nobody has asked the club for these yet. It is the single biggest risk to week four and it
needs no code — one evening with whoever keeps Caldecote's pictures. Until then the wall
shows the club's name, the countdown and whatever football has been typed in, and no photo
slides at all, which is correct rather than broken.

Landscape is better than portrait: the slide fills the screen with `object-fit: cover`, so
a tall photograph is cropped hard. Anything from a phone in the last ten years is more than
enough resolution for a television at six metres.
