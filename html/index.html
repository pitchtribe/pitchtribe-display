What PitchTribe does

Everything your club needs on screen

Noticeboards, fixtures, menus, guest Wi-Fi and 30 live widgets — all managed from a single web dashboard on any device connected to your club network.

Dynamic digital noticeboard

Publish announcements and news from your phone. Updates appear on screen within seconds — no print runs, no laminating.

  • Unlimited notice categories
  • Scheduled publish & expiry times
  • Rich text, images and pinned items
  • Live scrolling ticker strip

Scheduled fixtures & events

Pull in match schedules and club events. The display rotates through upcoming fixtures and updates when results come in.

  • Import from CSV or your league site
  • Matchday countdown timers
  • Multi-team and multi-sport support
  • Auto-archive past fixtures

Instant bar & kitchen menus

Change prices, mark items sold out, or switch to a matchday special in seconds from a simple dashboard.

  • Real-time price and availability updates
  • Multiple menu boards per screen
  • Scheduled menus (lunch, evening, matchday)
  • Allergen information display

Guest Wi-Fi captive portal

Turn your Pi into a Wi-Fi gateway. Visitors see a branded splash page before getting online — your logo, your message.

  • Custom splash page with club logo
  • Terms & acceptable-use acceptance
  • Per-session bandwidth controls
  • No third-party portal service needed

Live widgets & quizzes

Drop them into any playlist between your notices. Open data sources that allow club use, plus built-in packs that work without the internet.

  • ⚽ Live scores

Getting started

Set up in an afternoon

Three steps from unboxing to a live display. You need a Raspberry Pi 5 (4 GB+), a microSD card and an HDMI screen.

  1. 1

    Flash the SD card

    Download the PitchTribe image — Raspberry Pi OS Lite with PitchTribe already installed — and write it to the card with Raspberry Pi Imager (choose “Use custom”). If the Pi won’t be on a network cable, add your Wi-Fi in Imager’s settings before you write.

    Download the PitchTribe image →
  2. 2

    Plug in and switch on

    Connect the HDMI screen and power. The Pi boots straight to your display, and does on every boot, with no keyboard or mouse. Already have Raspberry Pi OS (Trixie or Bookworm) on the Pi? Skip the image and run this on it instead:

    curl -sSL https://pitchtribe.media/install.sh | sudo bash
  3. 3

    Manage from anywhere

    Open the dashboard on any phone, tablet or laptop on your club network. Update notices, menus and fixtures live — from the bar, the car park, wherever.

    http://pitchtribe.local

Never used a Raspberry Pi? Follow the step-by-step beginner’s guide — every click explained, no commands to type.

Built on solid foundations

Raspberry Pi 5.
Open source. Self-hosted.

PitchTribe runs entirely on hardware you own. No cloud subscription, no third-party dependency and no data leaving your site. The software is MIT-licensed and on GitHub.

Remote support is the one exception, and it is off on every card: only your club can switch it on, it pairs to your own Raspberry Pi account rather than ours, it is a terminal session with no access to your screen, and it switches itself off again after a time you choose — six hours by default — or the moment you end it.

Add roughly £4 for delivery. A 2 GB Pi 5 brings the build down to about £143 and an 8 GB board takes it to about £236. The network lead is optional — the Pi 5 has Wi-Fi built in.

Raspberry Pi board prices rose sharply through 2026 because of global memory shortages, so treat these as a snapshot rather than a firm quote. Budget for mounting hardware, a mains socket near the screen and a spare microSD card too.

Is it really free? +

The software is. PitchTribe is MIT-licensed open source, with no subscriptions, per-screen licences or hidden fees. You pay for the hardware once, and for the electricity it uses — a Pi 5 draws a few watts, though the TV it drives will be the larger share.

Does it need an internet connection? +

No. Displays and the dashboard work entirely over your local network. Internet is only needed for optional features like league fixture imports and software updates.

Remote support is the one exception, and it is off on every card: only your club can switch it on, it pairs to your own Raspberry Pi account rather than ours, it is a terminal session with no access to your screen, and it switches itself off again after a time you choose — six hours by default — or the moment you end it.

Can I run more than one screen? +

Yes. Each Pi 5 drives up to two displays, and you can add as many Pis as you like — manage them all from one dashboard, each showing its own playlist.
